# Incorrect fee

Trigger: fee-policy mismatch, stale preview, wrong assessment or aged receivable.

1. Locate the immutable policy and assessment snapshots.
2. Confirm scope precedence, effective interval, currency, rounding, min/max and deferral rule.
3. Do not edit policy history or assessed amounts.
4. Create an approved compensating adjustment/reversal with Payment reference.
5. Reconcile assessment, collection portions, adjustment and outstanding amount.

Success: every fee resolves to an approved snapshot and the remaining variance is zero.
